# Topology

- ENRE
  - Role: outage regulator for Edenor and Edesur
  - TUPA endpoint: `https://www.enre.gov.ar/paginacorte/js/data_EDN.js`
  - TUPA endpoint: `https://www.enre.gov.ar/paginacorte/js/data_EDS.js`
  - Documentation: `https://www.enre.gov.ar/mapacortes/index.html`
- CAMMESA
  - Role: demand data by region
  - TUPA endpoint: `https://api.cammesa.com/demanda-svc/demanda/`
  - TUPA regions: Edenor `1077`, Edesur `1078`, Edelap `1943`
  - Documentation: `https://cammesaweb.cammesa.com/informes-y-estadisticas/`
- OCEBA
  - Role: provincial regulator publishing EDELAP interruption data
  - TUPA endpoint: `https://oceba.gba.gov.ar/nueva_web/s.php?distribuidora=1&i=123`
  - Documentation: `https://oceba.gba.gov.ar/nueva_web/s.php?i=124`
